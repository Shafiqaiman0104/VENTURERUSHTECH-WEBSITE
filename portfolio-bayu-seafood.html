The Story Behind The Project

Bayu Seafood is an upscale lakeside dining establishment renowned for serving 100% Halal live seafood sourced directly from temperature-controlled marine tanks. Featuring seasonal delicacies such as Alaskan King Crabs, Boston Lobsters, and pristine ocean catches, Bayu Seafood pairs gourmet gastronomy with breathtaking lakeside vistas.

Beyond casual dining, the venue features exclusive private VIP suites and a grand banquet ballroom accommodating up to 80 guests. To deliver a digital presence matching their culinary luxury, Bayu Seafood commissioned VentureRush Tech to engineer three signature systems: an immersive 360° Interactive VR Tour allowing diners and corporate event organizers to inspect ballroom and VIP dining suites virtually, cinematic Scroll Driven Animation delivering a storytelling dining journey, and a dynamic Campaign & Content Managment System to update seasonal seafood menus, festival promos, and private event packages effortlessly.

The Challenge

  • Visualizing Tank Catches: Diners wanted to know available live seafood species, weights, and daily pricing before making the journey to the restaurant.
  • Event Space Under-Booking: Corporate planners were unaware of the ballroom's stage capabilities and AV equipment without physical site visits.
  • VIP Reservation Friction: Complex inquiries for customized banquet menus and seating configurations caused back-and-forth phone delays.

The VentureRush Solution

  • Interactive VR Tour: Implemented a 360° virtual tour allowing wedding planners and corporate organizers to explore VIP rooms and the Grand Ballroom virtually.
  • Scroll Driven Animation: Engineered fluid, cinematic scroll-driven visual transitions telling a rich culinary story as users browse tanks and venues.
  • Campaign & Content Managment System: Built a flexible content management system for restaurant staff to update seasonal catch market prices and launch festive dining campaigns.
