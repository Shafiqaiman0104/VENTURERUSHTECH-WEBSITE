01 / Case Study Overview

The story behind MK Rimba Camp Park.

Transforming a busy Johor eco-retreat into an automated 24/7 booking engine.

Engineering around real operational bottlenecks

Nestled in the lush natural landscapes of Pontian, MK Rimba Camp Park is a premier outdoor recreation and camping retreat. Featuring traditional Pondok A-Tents, dedicated campervan bays, scooter and ATV adventures, and event hosting spaces, the park attracts nature lovers, families, and road-trippers from across Malaysia and Singapore.

To streamline camp operations and meet skyrocketing weekend demand, MK Rimba Camp Park commissioned VentureRush Tech to engineer three integrated core systems: a 24/7 Custom AI Chatbot to answer camper inquiries and capture bookings around the clock, a centralized Campsite Management System governing lots and rental inventory, and a dedicated Agent & Commission Tracker empowering tourism partners with transparent affiliate tracking.

✕ Before VentureRush

The Business Challenge

  • ✕
    Delayed Staff Booking Responses: Campers inquiring late at night or during peak hours faced slow manual replies on WhatsApp, causing frustrated guests to abandon bookings.
  • ✕
    Manual Booking & Lot Clashes: Managing tent pitches, campervan bays, and rental equipment across manual spreadsheets led to double-booking conflicts and inventory gaps.
  • ✕
    Disorganized Partner Commissions: External travel agents and trip coordinators had no transparent portal to submit group reservations, resulting in commission disputes and slow payouts.
✦ The VentureRush Solution

Engineered to Convert & Scale

  • ✓
    24/7 Custom AI Chatbot: Engineered an intelligent conversational assistant trained on tent types, rates, facilities, and park rules to automate instant guest inquiries and bookings anytime.
  • ✓
    Campsite Management System: Built a unified operations dashboard for real-time tent lot reservations, campervan bay schedules, visitor check-ins, and gear inventory control.
  • ✓
    Agent & Commission Tracker: Created a dedicated B2B partner portal allowing travel agents to submit group bookings and monitor confirmed revenue and commission payouts transparently.
