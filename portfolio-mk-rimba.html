The Story Behind The Project

Nestled in the lush natural landscapes of Pontian, MK Rimba Camp Park is a premier outdoor recreation and camping retreat. Featuring traditional Pondok A-Tents, dedicated campervan bays, scooter and ATV adventures, and event hosting spaces, the park attracts nature lovers, families, and road-trippers from across Malaysia and Singapore.

To streamline camp operations and meet skyrocketing demand, MK Rimba Camp Park commissioned VentureRush Tech to build three core systems: a 24/7 Custom AI Chatbot to handle customer questions and booking inquiries around the clock, an automated Campsite Managment System to govern lots, inventory, and schedules, and a dedicated Agent & Commisson Tracker to empower tourism partners and track affiliate commissions seamlessly.

The Challenge

  • Inquiry Bottlenecks: Peak holiday inquiries flooded WhatsApp with repetitive questions regarding tent dimensions, rates, and amenities.
  • Late-Night Lost Bookings: Potential weekend campers planning trips late at night abandoned bookings due to delayed responses.
  • Unit Confusion: Guests were unsure about differences between A-Tents, campervan sites, and rental add-ons like portable cooking gear.

The VentureRush Solution

  • Custom AI Chatbot: Built an intelligent 24/7 conversational assistant trained on tent types, rates, facilities, and house rules to automate guest inquiry handling.
  • Campsite Managment System: Engineered a centralized operations engine for tent lot reservations, campervan bay schedules, and equipment inventory management.
  • Agent & Commisson Tracker: Developed a dedicated B2B agent portal allowing travel partners to register group bookings and track accrued commission payouts accurately.
