The Story Behind The Project

South Wave is the watersport arm of MK Marine & Yacht Service, operating at the world-class waterfront of Puteri Harbour Marina in Iskandar Puteri, Johor. From leisure family outings in whimsical Electric Swan and Car Boats to high-energy Single & Double Kayaking, Paddle Boats, and luxurious private yacht charters, South Wave provides unforgettable marine experiences.

During peak sunset hours and holiday weekends, dockside marshals faced long walk-in queues, manual paper waivers, and rental time confusion across numerous vessels. To transform waterfront operations, South Wave commissioned VentureRush Tech to build three custom digital solutions: a rapid Customer Order Hub for seamless walk-in and online reservations, a comprehensive Watersport Management System to track the active fleet, and a smart Rental Timer System to monitor watercraft sessions and ensure on-time dock returns.

The Challenge

  • Dockside Congestion: Manual ticketing created long queues at the Puteri Harbour dock during peak weekend afternoons.
  • Fleet Availability Conflicts: Popular vessels like the Electric Swan Boats experienced overlapping demand without real-time scheduling.
  • Unclear Rental Pricing: Tourists had difficulty understanding different rate tiers between manual kayaks and motorized electric boats.

The VentureRush Solution

  • Customer Order Hub: Built an intuitive digital booking terminal letting visitors select watercraft models, view hourly rates, and submit bookings with zero delay.
  • Watersport Management System: Engineered a unified fleet dashboard to monitor kayak, swan boat, electric car boat, and yacht availability in real time.
  • Rental Timer System: Implemented an automated rental session timer with visual cues to track boats out on the water and notify marshals upon due return.

Client-Requested Core Systems & Features

Customer Order Hub

Streamlined customer booking and order terminal allowing walk-ins and online visitors to select vessel models, review hourly rates, enter guest particulars, and confirm tickets in seconds.

Watersport Management System

End-to-end marina operations portal governing the entire watercraft fleet (Single/Double Kayaks, Swan Boats, Electric Cars, Yachts), berth allocations, and daily revenue statistics.

Rental Timer System

Intelligent active rental countdown system tracking time out on the harbour, calculating 30-min and 60-min sessions, and sending visual alerts when boats are due back at the pontoon.

Visual profiles for Single & Double Kayaks, Paddle Swan Boats, Electric Chinese Boats, and Electric Car Boats with clear specifications.
