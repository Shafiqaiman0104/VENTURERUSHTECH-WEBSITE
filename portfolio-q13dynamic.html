The Story Behind The Project

Q13 Dynamic is a premier brand specializing in celebratory pyrotechnics and festival fireworks. Their catalog includes hundreds of variations from high-altitude aerial shells and multi-shot repeaters to choreographed event barrages.

During peak holiday festival seasons, static catalog pictures failed to convey blast effects, sound power, and colors, causing customer support to be overloaded with repetitive calls. To revolutionize their business, Q13 Dynamic engaged VentureRush Tech to build three vital digital solutions: an automated Order Tracking system to provide instant shipment updates, a secure Agent Management Portal for wholesale distributors, and an all-in-one Fireworks Business Platform powered by demo videos and dynamic filtering.

The Challenge

  • Support Swamped by Order Inquiries: During peak festival seasons, customer support was overwhelmed by thousands of repetitive messages asking "Where is my parcel?" and "Has payment cleared?".
  • Messy Wholesale Agent Reordering: Wholesale agents and regional distributors had to submit bulk orders via chaotic chat messages without access to dealer tier pricing or automated bulk invoicing.
  • Blind Buying from Static Packaging: Customers struggled to visualize firework burst effects, colors, and durations from static box photos alone, lacking live-action demo videos and spec filters.

The VentureRush Solution

  • Order Tracking: Built an automated self-service lookup widget where customers enter their Order ID to verify payment, packing, and courier transit live.
  • Agent Management Portal: Developed a dedicated wholesale platform allowing registered distributor agents to submit bulk orders and access dealer pricing tiers.
  • Fireworks Business Platform: Engineered an enterprise commercial pyrotechnics platform featuring night-sky demo videos, multi-parameter spec filtering, and sub-second page loads.
