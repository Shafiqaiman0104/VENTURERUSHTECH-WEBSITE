VentureRush Tech respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information when you visit venturerushtech.com, request a website or custom system quotation, or engage our services across Johor Bahru, Malaysia, and Singapore. We handle personal data in accordance with Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and, where applicable, international data protection standards including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1

Information We Collect & Process

We do not require user accounts, passwords, or payment card details to browse our website. Information is collected only when you voluntarily interact with our site or contact our team:

  • Inquiry & Quotation Details: When you use our contact form, interactive View Price quotation form, WhatsApp, or email, you may provide your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, industry, estimated monthly revenue range, investment budget, preferred package, and project brief. On-page forms format these details directly into your chosen WhatsApp or email client so you stay in full control before sending.
  • Interactive On-Page Tools: Our ROI Sales Lift Simulator calculates estimates entirely within your browser and does not transmit your slider inputs to any server.
  • Free SEO, AEO & GEO Website Audits: When you enter a public website URL into our audit bar and click scan, your browser opens our companion diagnostic platform at sgachecker.venturerushtech.com to analyze publicly accessible pages on demand under its S.G.A Checker Privacy Policy.
  • Standard Technical Logs: Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure may temporarily process standard technical metadata (such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, and access timestamps) strictly to maintain site security, availability, and performance.
2

How We Use Your Information

Any personal or business information you voluntarily provide is used strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your project inquiries and providing tailored website, SEO/AEO/GEO, AI chatbot, or custom software quotations;
  • Scheduling and conducting free, zero-obligation digital growth consultations;
  • Delivering contracted web design, custom software development, API integration, and technical support services;
  • Fulfilling invoicing, commercial documentation, and statutory record-keeping requirements under Malaysian law.
We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
3

WhatsApp, Email & Third-Party Services

Our website includes direct links to WhatsApp (+60 11-6080 1477), email (venturerush.dev@gmail.com), live client portfolio platforms, and our free diagnostic tool (sgachecker.venturerushtech.com). When you initiate communication via WhatsApp, email, or visit an external website, that platform’s own privacy policy applies alongside our commitment to keep your inquiry strictly confidential.

4

Cookies & Third-Party Assets

venturerushtech.com does not deploy intrusive cross-site advertising trackers or require login cookies. Standard web typography assets (such as Google Fonts) may be loaded by your browser to ensure crisp visual presentation. You can manage or restrict cookies and cached data at any time through your browser settings.

5

Data Security & Retention

We apply reasonable administrative, organizational, and technical safeguards to protect client communications and project records against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Inquiry records are retained only for as long as necessary to address your consultation, deliver contracted services, or comply with applicable Malaysian statutory and SSM business record obligations.

6

Your Rights Under PDPA & GDPR

Under Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)—and the GDPR for visitors in applicable jurisdictions—you have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information;
  • Withdraw consent or request deletion of your inquiry details where no ongoing contractual or legal retention obligation applies.
7

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to our digital platforms or regulatory updates. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date at the top.
